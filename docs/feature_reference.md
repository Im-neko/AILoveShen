## 構成要素

AILoveShen is an AI Streamer project for **Twitch** combining:
- **[Jev](https://typesafe.ai/)**: TypeSafe AI's System One model — picks the next primitive action among the candidates the [Mineflayer](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/mineflayer) Node.js bridge in `minecraft-bridge/` grounds for the current goal (replaces the earlier NitroGen plan; see `docs/design/06_phase6_jev_integration.md` and `docs/design/11_primitive_actions.md`)
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash** (`main_model`): Game commentary & thoughts (main), comment responses (sub/interrupt), and high-level Goal/direction decisions that get handed to Jev
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash** (`filter_model`, planned): Comment filtering (dynamic threshold based on volume)
- **Style-Bert-VITS2**: BERT-based TTS with emotional style control (JP/EN/ZH)
- **Irodori-TTS** (optional, `tts.engine: irodori` / `TTS_ENGINE`): Japanese TTS cloning the voice from reference audio without training (`IrodoriTtsClient` → Irodori-TTS-Server `POST /v1/audio/speech` on :8088; on the Mac it runs natively for MPS, not in Docker: `scripts/irodori/setup_mac.sh` / `start_mac.sh`; reference clips from the Style-Bert-VITS2 training data by `tools/irodori_voice.py`; `tools/tts_compare.py` compares engines; fixed `seed`, emotion captions off by default; `tools/irodori_from_sbv2.py` makes a clean reference voice or a LoRA (`tts.irodori.lora_adapter`) from Style-Bert-VITS2's readings of the recording script, and per-emotion voices (`emotions`: Irodori-TTS reads the script's emotion lines with captions → `tts.irodori.emotion_voices`, and `tools/sbv2_add_styles.py` adds them as Style-Bert-VITS2 styles, `neutral` (read without a caption) remakes Neutral and keeps the former as `Neutral-01` (run with the repo's `.venv`: `Style-Bert-VITS2/venv`'s torchaudio 2.9 breaks pyannote); `tts.synthesis.style_weight`; to retrain Style-Bert-VITS2 on clean audio: `irodori_from_sbv2.py generate --engine irodori` reads the script, `voice_trim_tail.py` cuts the noise Irodori leaves after every line (its `trim_tail` only cuts a perfectly flat latent), `voice_screen.py` lists speaker-embedding outliers (a hint only: it also reacts to prosody), `voice_dataset.py --neutral-only --extra Style=folder` builds `Data/<model>/`, then Colab (`docs/voice/README.md`, 2026-10-02)); `docs/setup/irodori_tts.md`, design 32)
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)**: Memory management, expression control, and extensibility


## メインループ・サブループと、LLM → Jev → Minecraft ブリッジの 3 層

- **Main loop**: Minecraft Bridge (Mineflayer) plays game → Gemini 3.8 Flash generates commentary/thoughts → TTS speaks
- **Sub loop**: Twitch comments → Gemini 3.8 Flash filters → Gemini 3.8 Flash responds (interrupts main)
- **Three-layer LLM → Jev → Minecraft Bridge link** (`GameService.play`; design: `docs/design/10_agent_lifecycle.md`, `11_primitive_actions.md`):
  - LLM: Gemini designs the house (JSON blueprint validated by `HouseBlueprint`) and sets goals in a predicate vocabulary (`have(item, n)`, `built`, `placed(item, where, count)` (any placeable item: the home's furniture bed / crafting_table / furnace / chest in its room, or anything (torch, lantern, …) `where` = `home` / `near_home` / `build:<name>` / `x,y,z`, counted in that area from the world: bridge `placing.mjs`, leaf `place_at` with the face to place against (`supportFor`: torches, signs, banners and ladders go on a wall first, in the home at floor + 1; never against chests, tables, doors or beds, which would open); the tool `place {item, x, y, z, on?: floor|wall|ceiling}` may put anything in the home's room except the doorway column and the bed's room), `at_home`, `through_night`, `explored(distance)`, `cleared` (by day: fight what waits at the door), `stored(item, n)` (in the home's chests)); the house is designed only when there is no home yet (a home keeps its name and design across restarts); a goal the bridge rejects goes back with the reason
  - Recipes (`docs/design/29_recipes.md`): every vanilla 1.21.4 recipe and item tag is bundled in `minecraft-bridge/data-static/` (`npm run recipes:fetch`, from misode/mcmeta); `RecipeIndex` (`src/recipes.mjs`) expands tags and searches; `Knowledge` plans crafting (as `#tag` / `any:` groups; same-kind recipes like dyeing only from the white one) and smelting from it; tools `recipe_of` / `find_recipes` (also `t.find_recipes` in skills). Crafting itself goes through the server's recipe book (all recipes are given on RCON connect), so Gemini may name any craftable item it knows
  - Minecraft Bridge: judges the goal from the world (never from the models), decomposes it with a dependency solver over minecraft-data (recipes, drops, with corrections), grounds concrete candidates (dig this block, craft that item, ...) plus what the body needs, removes unsafe ones (nothing outside while sheltering; what is held back goes to the goal's `blocked`, and by day a wall exit is offered), and runs one primitive (aborted on damage). A reflex handles nearby hostiles and keeps the bot afloat
  - World memory (`docs/design/14_world_memory.md`, bridge `memory.mjs`, saved in `state.json`): only what was seen and is out of view now, with when (places per 16x16 region, explored regions, deaths, chest contents as last opened). Recalled places are offered as trips before blind exploration, the solver takes stored items from a chest before gathering, and `/observe` summarises it for the prompts and the goal board
  - Death recovery (`docs/design/50_goal_layers.md` §17, 2026-09-29): what the streamer carries drops where it died and despawns after 5 minutes (three deaths in a run left it with nothing: no tools, no wood, no food). `recoverableDeath` (`memory.mjs`, up to 4.5 minutes after the death, not recovered, under 2 tries) makes `forNeeds` offer `recover your things where you died at x,y,z` (`recover_items`: walk there, pick up the drops within 24 m; a death with nothing left to pick up is given up; the id has no distance or time, which change with every step and made the chosen id refused, 2026-09-30); `StepPicker` runs it without asking Jev before anything else, even after a failed step (the last step before a death is usually `stopped: health critical`), and `AdvancePlayUseCase._recover_first` runs it right after a death before the next small goal is decided (48 s passed in the 2026-09-30 log: two goal decisions and a wasted Gemini step; not counted as a step of any goal); it is not offered while sheltering (night) and is not stopped by dusk (`NIGHT_VERBS`)
  - Surroundings (`minecraft-bridge/src/landmarks.mjs`): `observation.nearby` lists beds, doors, crafting tables, furnaces, chests and torches within 24 m (count, nearest with distance and direction, owner home / former_home / none), shown in `activity()` as 「近くにあるもの」. With no home yet, a door with a roofed, closed room behind it (≤ 100 cells) is adopted as the home (`adoptFoundBase`, named 見つけた拠点), so a base the bot did not build is used as a temporary shelter, not as a house of its own (`home.found`, saved; older saves without it are recognised by the name): `placed(bed | crafting_table | furnace | chest, home)` counts only furniture the bot placed itself there (`state.placed` now records furniture as well as logs; `ownFurnitureInHome`), and the state line says `仮の宿` (`docs/design/62_found_base.md`, 2026-09-30, unverified on the real server)
  - What is in front (`docs/design/45_what_is_in_front.md`, bridge `around.mjs`): `observation.around` lists the exposed blocks within 8 m horizontally and feet ±2 (per kind: count, nearest with coordinates / distance / direction / height, `gives`, ripe crops — a crop's nearest is the nearest ripe one, and `null` (no coordinates, shown as 育ち途中) when none is ripe, because unripe ones are refused; 24 kinds, nearest first) and the dropped items in the same space, shown every time as 「目の前のブロック」「目の前に落ちている物」; the tool rules say to use them before searching far
  - Planting and farming (`docs/design/33_planting_and_farming.md`, bridge `farming.mjs`): `planted(sapling|kind, n)` counts saplings or grown trees where the bot planted (`state.plantings`), `farmed(crop, n)` crops on the farms: the designed farms for that crop plus the home farm (farmland near the home outside designed farms), summed, the last seen counts when not loaded (`facilities.mjs` `cropFarms`, `state.memory.farms[facility][crop]`) (wheat / carrots / potatoes / beetroots); both are conditions and skill expects. New farmland and saplings go only in designed facilities' cells (design 47 §14): tools and primitives `till` / `plant` refuse other cells, and without x,y,z take the next cell (`workCell`: the small goal's facility, else that crop's / sapling's facilities; `sow` also the home farm's free farmland; only as many cells are tilled as there are seeds to sow while no water reaches (`cropFarms` `sow` = tilled and empty; `till` is refused and the solver / goal leaves omit it otherwise): unplanted farmland turns back to dirt without water within 4 blocks, 2026-09-29; a designed farm has water cells inside its rectangle, one per 9x9 (`layout.water`, design 47 §17): `facility` puts water first (`place_water` / tool `water`: dig the cell, use the water_bucket on the hole's floor), the solver makes a water_bucket from a bucket (3 iron) and a water source (`fill` = `use_item`), unverified on the real server); with no farm or tree farm, or no room for n, the goal says to add one with a new name, `facility(<new name>)` (a facility cannot be enlarged) (`impossible` when nothing is left to do); `dig` also takes grass, crops and saplings. Saplings come from leaves and seeds from grass (chance drops: nothing dropped is not a failure). Crops are gathering sources (`NATURAL_BLOCKS`), only ripe ones are dug (`isRipe`; the tool `dig` refuses unripe ones, and farmland itself unless `demolish: true`, 2026-09-29: Gemini called it with the coordinates under a wheat and turned the farm into dirt), a ripe crop's `dig` harvests the ripe ones within 5 m and replants them with the seeds picked up; world memory remembers crops; `/observe` gives `farm` (per crop: planted, ripe, ages, free cells, farm names → 「畑」) and each remembered place's `gives` (`Knowledge.dropsOf`: what the block drops, e.g. wheat → wheat, wheat_seeds); `find_blocks` marks crops `ripe` / `age`. The farms (`cropFarms`) come first: with a crop not in sight, ripe ones there → only `go to the farm`, growing ones → no far trip or exploring, a `growing` reason in `blocked` / `impossible` (crops grow only near a player: stay near and do other steps; bone meal if carried). The procedure is left to Gemini's skills in tools mode; candidates mode has the basic plant / sow / till steps. While the farms grow, the `growing` leaf offers sow / till (`also`) for their free cells (with no seeds: dig grass for them; with no hoe: make one — before, a broken hoe and used-up seeds left only `wait` for minutes, 2026-09-29) and suggests `farmed(crop, planted + free)`, or, with no free cell, adding another farm with a new name
  - Gathering: `stored(item, n)` carries items home in batches (deposit only at 32 carried, enough to finish, a nearly full inventory, near the home, nothing left to gather here, or dusk/night), and every goal is sent with `also` (items the other objectives and the current jobs gather: have / stored / planted → saplings / farmed → seeds): gather candidates for them within 8 m are offered by day, marked `also` (a tree gives logs and, from its leaves, saplings); such side work is not counted as a stall

Eating (2026-10-03, `docs/design/84_supply_tiers.md` §9, user: 「食料の必要量の見積もりが甘い…HPが減ってる時は満タンにしないと意味ない」): the hunger points per item are minecraft-data's `foodsByName[x].foodPoints` (potato 1, raw pork 3, bread 5, cooked beef 8; full = 20). `forNeeds` picks what to eat by least waste (`points − (20 − food)`, then the larger), and offers an item wasting more than `EAT_WASTE_OK` (3) only when starving (food ≤ 6) or hurt; one `eat` keeps eating the same item until food 20 (`primitives.mjs`); with health ≤ `HEAL_BELOW` (16) and food < 18 (`healing.mjs` `needsFoodToHeal`) it eats up to 18 even wasting points (`healTo`), `urgent` when no hostile is near (health ≤ 8: always), raw meat too when nothing can cook it; `needs()` says `health N/20 and food F/20: it heals only while food is 18 or more` (never the word "hunger": Python reads it as hungry)
  - Food: cooked food is eaten first; raw meat only when starving (food ≤ 6) or when nothing can cook it (≤ 12); raw meat is cooked at a visible furnace or walked to the last seen one (`state.lastFurnace`, 3+ pieces).
